About this data

Source: UN Comtrade, annual goods trade reported by India (reporter code 699), Harmonized System classification. Exports are valued FOB and imports CIF, as is standard. Product groups are HS 2-digit chapters with shortened labels; full detail is in the CSV downloads. Values are current US dollars, not adjusted for inflation. Figures can differ slightly from India’s Ministry of Commerce statistics because of timing, exchange-rate conversion and revisions.
