About this data

Source: UN Comtrade. India’s supplier chart uses India’s reported imports (CIF) by HS 2-digit chapter and partner country. The world chart ranks the 16 largest exporter countries by their own reported exports (FOB) of the chapter. A few country-chapter cells are unavailable on the free API tier and are omitted rather than estimated. Every chart downloads as CSV.
