About this data

Source: UN Comtrade, annual imports reported by the United States (reporter 842) and the European Union (reporter 97, extra-EU trade), valued CIF in current US dollars. Value chains are defined by HS 2-digit chapters: textiles & apparel = 50–63; pharmaceuticals = 30; leather & footwear = 41–43 and 64; electronics = 85. EU customs-procedure sub-records are aggregated to totals. Every chart’s underlying numbers can be downloaded as CSV.
