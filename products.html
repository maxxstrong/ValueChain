About this data

Source: UN Comtrade. The trend chart uses India’s own reported exports and imports by HS 2-digit chapter. The market chart uses each importer’s reported imports of the chapter — total and from India — for its latest available year (EU figures are extra-EU trade). Values are current US dollars. Every chart downloads as CSV.
