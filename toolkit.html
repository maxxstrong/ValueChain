About this tool

The market ranking is computed from this site’s UN Comtrade data — each market’s reported imports of your product group, and how much of that came from India. Agreement status comes from the trade agreements data. Scheme entries are curated from official sources, each carrying the date it was last checked and a link to the authority that runs it.

This tool deliberately does not show duty rates. Tariffs vary by 8-digit HS code, by origin certificate and by agreement schedule, and a number that is nearly right is worse than no number. Use the Indian Trade Portal or the government’s TIA portal for duties, and treat everything here as orientation rather than advice.
